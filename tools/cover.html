Make a cover for your post.

Every Macedonia post gets a photo at the top. This makes yours match the rest of the website in about a minute.

  1. Choose a photo, or start with one of the church photos below.
  2. Tap the most important part of the photo, usually someone's face. That part stays in view on every screen.
  3. Download the cover, then upload it as your post's photo in Nucleus.

Leave words off the photo. Nucleus puts your post title on top of it for you.
